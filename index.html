About RG Worm MOW

RG Worm MOW calculates the measurement over wires of worm threads for a given axial tooth thickness, and the pre-plate measurement over wires for a minimum and maximum plating allowance.

Attribution: RG Worm MOW reproduces the measurement-over-wires method of the DOS program Quick Worm (QWORM, © 1995 James R. Shaneyfelt), which Ram-Gear has used, and its results were checked against that program’s outputs. It is an independent re-implementation by Ram-Gear Manufacturing Inc. It contains no code from the original and is not affiliated with or endorsed by its author.

Formulas

  • Lead L = N × P; threads per inch = N ÷ L; axial pitch P = 1 ÷ TPI (single start)
  • Pitch diameter D = L ÷ (π × tan λ)
  • Axial pressure angle tan φa = tan φn ÷ cos λ
  • Best-size wire W = (0.5 × P) × cos λ ÷ cos φn
  • Contributing backlash B = P/2 − axial tooth thickness
  • Plating → backlash Bp = 2 × plating ÷ (cos φn × cos λ)
  • Reduced pitch diameter D′ = D − (B + Bp) × cot φa, lead angle re-evaluated at D′: tan λ′ = L ÷ (π D′)
  • Helix angle ψ = 90° − λ′; transverse PA tan φt = tan φn ÷ cos ψ; base diameter Db = D′ cos φt; base helix sin ψb = sin ψ × cos φn
  • inv φw = π/(2N) + inv φt + W ÷ (Db cos ψb) − π/N   (inv x = tan x − x)
  • Measurement over wires M = Db ÷ cos φw + W

See notes.md in the project for where each formula came from and how it was checked against the original.
